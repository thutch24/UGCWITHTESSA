Meet the mum behind the camera

A bit about the woman behind the chaos.

I'm a mum, wife, dog mum of two and a serial hobby collector who has never once mastered the art of doing things in moderation.

One minute I'm transforming a corner of my house, the next I'm finding something that makes parenting easier, and somewhere in between I've decided to film the entire thing.

My content is an extension of who I am. Expressive, conversational, occasionally chaotic and never short of something to say. I want people watching my videos to feel like they're getting a recommendation from a mate, not sitting through a commercial break.

And yes, I absolutely talk this much in real life.

More than just a mum with a camera

There's a method to the madness.

As chaotic as I might appear, there's a reason behind what I create. I know how to grab someone's attention, keep them watching and introduce a product without making the whole thing feel like one big sales pitch.

Personality isn't an optional extra.

Anyone can hold up a product and list its features. I give people a reason to care about them.

Real life is my filming studio.

An actual family home, actual routines and actual everyday problems. Your product gets to be part of the story, not just a prop.

Creative freedom, without the guesswork.

I'll bring the ideas, the energy and the execution while keeping your brand's goals in mind.
