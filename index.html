How I Think

When the brief needs strategy,
this is how I think.

Not every piece of content needs a problem-solution script. Organic B-roll can build desire, a showcase can make features clear, and an unboxing can create anticipation. But when a brief needs a conversion-led angle, I find the real customer moment behind the product. The format follows the job.

Baby & family · Toddler tower

“This toddler tower has been a GAME CHANGER.”

“Trying to cook dinner with a toddler attached to your leg? This is how mine finally joined in without me growing a third arm.”

Why it works: It opens on the exact 5pm chaos a parent already knows. The tower becomes the resolution, not the headline.

Home · Cleaning product

“You need this amazing mop in your life.”

“If getting out the mop and bucket feels like a whole event, this is the version you can grab for the mess under the high chair and be done in minutes.”

Why it works: It names the effort that makes people put off cleaning, gives the product a specific everyday job and makes the time-saving benefit easy to picture.

Sometimes the hook is a human truth. Sometimes the product just needs to look really good. I know the difference.
